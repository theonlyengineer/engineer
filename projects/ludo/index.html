Ludo Jaldi: Play with Friends

Last updated 2026-11-07

Android game · The classic Ludo board game: share one phone, play the computer, or play online.

Ludo Jaldi is a Ludo board game for Android: share one phone with friends, play against the computer, or play online. It is free and shows ads; online play uses an anonymous guest profile and an optional Google sign-in.

Ludo Jaldi is in testing and is not on Google Play yet.

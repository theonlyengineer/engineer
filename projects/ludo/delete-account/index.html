Ludo Jaldi: Data and account deletion

Last updated 2026-11-07

Google Play requires every developer to publish a route for deleting an account and the data behind it. This is ours. It is short, because there is very little to delete.

Data

Our published apps store their data on your device. Where a copy can also be in your own Android backup, or where an app's ads send data to Google, that app's section says so.

Ludo Jaldi

Ludo Jaldi's local games live in a single file in the app's private storage, and uninstalling it removes that file. Its online play keeps records on Google's Firebase service.

  • In the app: open Settings, then Online, then Delete my online profile. This deletes your player record (your display name and when you were last seen) and your sign-in account, and signs you out. A guest account is deleted at once.
  • If you signed in with Google and the app cannot finish deleting your account without asking you to sign in again, or if you have already uninstalled the app, write to nizar@theonlyengineer.com with the display name you used and, if you can, the approximate time you played. We will delete the account and its record, and tell you when it is done.
  • Game rooms: a room you played in holds a display name and moves. Rooms are marked to expire a day after last use and are removed by the app when someone opens online play; you can also ask us to remove one.
  • Ad data: held by Google, not by us. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings, and change your consent choice through Privacy options in Settings where it is shown.

Asking us directly

If you believe we hold information about you and you want it deleted, write to nizar@theonlyengineer.com with the app's name and enough detail to identify what you are asking about. We will respond within 30 days.

Because none of our apps sends us an identifier (an advertising ID collected for ads goes to Google, not to us), we generally have no way to look you up — which is also the reason there is nothing of yours for us to lose. If we hold nothing, we will tell you that plainly rather than sending a form response.

Purchases

If any of our apps ever sells a subscription, the subscription itself is held by Google Play, not by us. Cancelling or requesting a refund is done through Google Play → Subscriptions, and deleting data with us does not cancel a Google Play subscription. See Google's instructions.

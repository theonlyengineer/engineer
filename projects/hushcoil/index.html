Hushcoil: Snake Puzzle

Last updated 2026-11-07

Android game · A snake-escape logic puzzle: tap a snake and it slithers out head-first.

Hushcoil is a snake-escape logic puzzle for Android. Tap a snake and it slithers out head-first, as long as nothing lies in its path; if another snake is in the way, it strikes and the bitten snake dies. Boards take shapes, snakes cross over and under each other, and there are 300 levels that grow steadily harder, endless encore levels after them, and a new daily coil every day.

Hushcoil is in testing and is not on Google Play yet.
